## Authentication

`docs/authentication.md`. Better Auth 1.7.13 with the Drizzle SQLite adapter on D1. Signed bearer tokens (7 days, revocable in D1), Keychain on iOS and an `httpOnly` cookie behind the web proxy. Per-IP fixed-window rate limits in D1 return `429`. Registration can be closed from the admin panel; iOS and web then offer an early-access waitlist (stored in D1, reviewed in the admin panel) and a link to self-host. Email verification and password reset are not configured.

People can **delete their own account** from Account on iOS and web. The confirmation lists what is removed, including owned groups and created contacts for everyone else, and requires the current password. `DELETE /api/v1/me` checks it (a wrong one is `403`, never `401`), allows five attempts per minute, refuses super admins and then runs the same cascade and durable R2/Workflow cleanup as the admin panel (migration 0069), returning `202`.

## Onboarding circle

`docs/onboarding.md`. `POST /api/v1/onboarding` freezes the person's answers in `account_onboarding` and starts one `OnboardingWorkflow` per account. Muse Spark plans **five contacts** in one of six visual styles (including 3D animated pets or a custom theme), they are published immediately with `setup_pending = 1`, portraits are drawn in parallel with `muse-image-1.0` into R2, and an optional Friends, Study or Work group is created at the end. The person enters the app while contacts are still being created; the Cron recovers stalled runs. Migrations 0041, 0069 and 0077.

## AI contact generation

`docs/contact-generation.md`. `POST /api/v1/contacts/generation` creates an idempotent job (migration 0052) that runs on `ONBOARDING_WORKFLOW` with 2-minute text and 4-minute portrait budgets, publishing atomically. Create contact is one form: an empty name is generated, and the photo is generated, uploaded (staged in R2 until publish, migration 0095) or omitted; the personality is always generated. Creators can also generate a new photo for an existing contact (`POST /api/v1/contacts/:id/photo/generate`, appearance derived from the saved identity). It reuses onboarding context and can regenerate an existing personality as a reviewable draft. Generated contacts get random adult birthdays (ages clamped to 18–120).

## Model settings

`docs/model-settings.md`. Each account chooses **Standard** (`muse-spark-1.3`) or **Contributor** (`muse-spark-1.3-contributor`) with explicit consent (`PUT /api/v1/me/model`, column `user.chat_model`, migration 0028). Each Workflow fixes its model at start; groups use the requesting human's model; chats with MCP connectors always use Standard. The admin flag `contributor_only` temporarily processes every account with Contributor without changing saved choices.

## Contacts

`docs/contacts.md`. Contacts live in D1 (migration 0002) and are always scoped by owner. Lists use cursor paging by `lastMessageAt`. Private aliases (0075) and **linked contacts** (`contact_link`, 0078) let two of a person's contacts reach each other, except connector-isolated ones. Topic recall ranks earlier private and group history before each reply.

## Profile photos

`docs/profile-photos.md`. Clients crop to a 512×512 JPEG (about 500 KiB) stored in R2 with versioned keys (migrations 0006 and 0015). Contacts can look at their own photo, the person's and current group members' through a read-only `view_profile_photo` tool that rechecks access and version. Photos also appear as notification avatars through signed URLs.

## Shared contacts

`docs/sharing.md`. A creator shares a contact by account email (in-app request) or a 24-hour invitation code (migrations 0013 and 0014). One agent identity serves several people, but each participant has an **isolated private conversation**; schedules, unread counts, attachments and notifications stay per recipient. Participants can ask the shared contact to relay a private message to another participant (`send_private_message`, migration 0084) with requester attribution and one-send receipts. iOS discloses shared-memory and Contributor data use before acceptance.

## Personality

`docs/personality.md`. Creators edit a concise identity, specialties, birthday and time zone (computed age) and **seventeen temperament traits** on a 1–10 scale (`shared/personality.ts`), with compare-and-set revisions (migrations 0039 and 0040). The contact receives behavioral direction rather than raw scores; a server-side acting director carries relationships across eligible chats without exposing private causes. Personality events older than 60 days are pruned by the Cron.

## Conversation style

`docs/conversation-style.md`. Server-side prompt direction for natural regional wording (speech accent from the contact or its origin), distinct per-contact writing habits seeded by contact ID, brief replies and group addressing. No migration. A live evaluation report lives in `docs/conversation-style-evaluation.md`.
