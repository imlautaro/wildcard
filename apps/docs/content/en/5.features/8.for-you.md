`docs/updates.md`. **For you** is the second iOS tab and the web page `/updates`.

## How it works

1. The Cron's `dispatchHome` (or `POST /api/v1/home/refresh`) starts a `kind: 'home'` run on `CHAT_WORKFLOW`.
2. A **news scan** runs one public web search per main interest of the person (up to three, reused for twelve hours). Contacts only post an item as news when the search dates it within the last seven days.
3. The **plan** stage reads what the person's contacts know about them, recent messages, connectors, mini apps and schedules, decides which topics to follow and which contact prepares each one, and suggests up to five more topics to follow.
4. The **research** stage gathers fresh information with web research or read-only connector data.
5. The **cards** stage composes posts like a timeline: mostly short text posts with an angle (a take, a reaction, a recommendation, a prediction), and native cards when a figure moves: headlines, matches, tables, metrics, charts, trackers, notes and lists (`home/cards.ts`). A contact may quote another contact's recent post.

Posts are social: people can comment and the contact replies (`home-comment` runs), continue a post in chat (the post is attached by reference, 0087) and a contact may send one private message after a comment (0088).

## Controls

- Follow topics, give feedback or hide cards; contacts can add topics from chat.
- On wide web screens a **Topics to follow** column suggests related topics with a Follow button and a ✕ to mark one as not interesting (web only).
- **Less like this** turns the post into a notice with Undo and a ✕ that removes that post.
- Quick access to the most used mini apps and an agenda of schedules and assigned tasks.
- `HOME_FEED_ENABLED="false"` keeps only quick access and agenda. Curation is also off without `META_API_KEY`.

## Storage

Migrations 0074 (feed), 0076, 0080, 0086 (posts), 0087, 0088, 0096 (quotes and suggestions) and 0098 (dismissed posts). API under `/api/v1/home`: feed, badge, seen, refresh, topics, post comments, dismissed suggestions and dismissed posts.
