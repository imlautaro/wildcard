All media bytes live in the private R2 bucket and are served only through authorized API routes (see [R2 storage](/en/cloudflare/r2)).

## Attachments

[`docs/attachments.md`](/en/reference/attachments). One JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP or PDF per message, up to 5 MiB, sent base64-encoded in the turn body, stored in R2 (migration 0012) and read by the model as part of the turn. Clients show native previews; the web app accepts drag and drop. The Cron removes orphaned objects.

## Voice messages

[`docs/voice.md`](/en/reference/voice). Hold to record, release to send, slide to cancel or lock. Clients send 16 kHz WAV; the Workflow transcribes it with `muse-voice-transcribe-1.0` and stores audio in R2 (migration 0060). Contacts can answer with their own **ElevenLabs** voice (`eleven_v4`) when `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` is configured, the admin setting `voice_messages_enabled` is on and the contact opted in; creators choose a speech accent (0061, including Argentine). Listening state and duration are tracked per account (0066). iOS supports background playback, system media controls and proximity receiver routing.

## Generated images

[`docs/generated-images.md`](/en/reference/generated-images). The `generate_chat_image` tool calls `muse-image-1.0` (generations or edits) and stores results in R2. Framing (portrait, landscape, square) is chosen from context; selfies use the contact's avatar and named members use theirs (up to three authorized reference photos plus earlier images from the same chat). Internal visual descriptions let contacts find earlier scenes (`search_chat_images`, `view_chat_image`). Albums of two or three images ship on one message (0082). Contacts can send private spontaneous images within initiative limits (0079). Migrations 0044 and 0048.

## Stickers

[`docs/stickers.md`](/en/reference/stickers). `POST /api/v1/stickers/cutout` sends an image to Meta `sam-3.1`, parses the streamed mask with `@meta-sam/parser` and builds a transparent PNG in pure JavaScript (`jpeg-js`, `upng-js`), with a Muse Spark fallback that names the subject. Stickers carry an emoji, live in a personal library usable in every chat, can be saved from contacts and "stolen" by contacts, and a personality trait controls how often a contact sends them (0091, 0092).

## Link previews

[`docs/link-previews.md`](/en/reference/link-previews). `GET /api/v1/link-previews` fetches the page head under `global_fetch_strictly_public`, extracts metadata (with YouTube and Vimeo oEmbed) and caches the image in R2 under `link-previews/<hash>` (0073). The Cron expires old previews.
