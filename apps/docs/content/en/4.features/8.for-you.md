`docs/updates.md`. **For you** is the second iOS tab and the web page `/updates`.

## How it works

1. The Cron's `dispatchHome` (or `POST /api/v1/home/refresh`) starts a `kind: 'home'` run on `CHAT_WORKFLOW`.
2. The **plan** stage reads what the person's contacts know about them, recent messages, connectors, mini apps and schedules, decides which topics to follow and which contact prepares each one.
3. The **research** stage gathers fresh information with web research or read-only connector data.
4. The **cards** stage composes native cards: headlines, matches, tables, metrics, charts, trackers, notes and lists (`home/cards.ts`).

Posts are social: people can comment and the contact replies (`home-comment` runs), continue a post in chat (the post is attached by reference, 0087) and a contact may send one private message after a comment (0088).

## Controls

- Follow topics, give feedback or hide cards; contacts can add topics from chat.
- Quick access to the most used mini apps and an agenda of schedules and assigned tasks.
- `HOME_FEED_ENABLED="false"` keeps only quick access and agenda. Curation is also off without `META_API_KEY`.

## Storage

Migrations 0074 (feed), 0076, 0080, 0086 (posts), 0087 and 0088. API under `/api/v1/home`: feed, badge, seen, refresh, topics and post comments.
