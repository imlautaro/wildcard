Contacts can do work beyond replying. Every capability runs inside the existing D1, Workflows and Cron, with explicit budgets.

## Tasks and persistent work

- `docs/tasks.md`: shared task lists in private chats and groups (migration 0046). Contacts and people create, assign, block and cancel tasks; an assigned contact works in its own `CHAT_WORKFLOW` turn, again when its blockers finish and in periodic reviews. Tasks can link a deliverable document, and reviewers can send work back at most twice (0058).
- `docs/persistent-work.md`: `workState`, `nextStep` and `evidence` with pause, resume and cancel on both clients (0054). Budget: 6 work turns per task plus 30 per contact per day. A task may run one human-saved exact public web query with the app closed (0055).

## Documents

`docs/documents.md`. Markdown documents that contacts and people write together, opened full screen with a viewer and editor. Edits use compare-and-set revisions (the last 50 are kept) through `create_document` / `edit_document` (0047).

## Web research

`docs/web-research.md`. `research_web` calls Meta Responses with `web_search` (`store: false`). Results inform the conversation, or are saved with sources and provenance in a document when a lasting report was requested. Clients show cited-source counts, check dates and source panels. Durable replay receipts and rolling limits (10 per account and 100 per installation per day). Gated by `WEB_RESEARCH_ENABLED`. Schedules, ordinary bot triggers and connector-isolated chats cannot research. Migrations 0053, 0055, 0068, 0071, 0072.

## Page monitors

`docs/monitors.md`. Attached to a contact's task (0056). The Cron fetches the public page, hashes and diffs it **in code**, and only wakes the contact for new relevant changes. Limits: 20 monitors per person and 20 change turns per task.

## Mini apps

`docs/mini-apps.md`. In private chats a contact can build declarative tools and dashboards (`create_mini_app`): counters, stats, lists, charts, actions, tabs, menus, composers and lessons, rendered natively with SwiftUI and web components. Specs are validated and verified offline (automatic checks plus the contact's tests) before saving (`miniapps/spec.ts`, `miniapps/verify.ts`). Apps that read MCP tools stay disabled until the person reviews those tools. Migrations 0037, 0038, 0074.

## MCP connectors

`docs/connectors.md`. People configure remote MCP servers once in **Account → Connections** (from a library of official servers such as Notion, Linear, GitHub, Stripe and Google Workspace, or manually) and grant them per contact or per group.

- Transport: Streamable HTTP with the MCP SDK 1.32.2 on the existing Worker (no Durable Objects).
- Auth: public, Bearer, or OAuth authorization code with PKCE, registered or dynamic clients and refresh tokens. Credentials are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from `BETTER_AUTH_SECRET`.
- Egress is restricted to public addresses (DNS-over-HTTPS guard plus `global_fetch_strictly_public`).
- A chat with a connector is permanently isolated from cross-chat memory and uses the Standard model.
- Migrations 0026, 0027, 0029. OAuth needs `MCP_WEB_ORIGIN` and public HTTPS origins.

## Schedules

`docs/schedules.md`. `create_schedule` writes `contact_schedule` (0011); the Cron's `dispatchSchedules` starts due items (50 per run). Limits: 5 per contact and 20 per person, intervals from 10 minutes to 30 days. Contacts can schedule for another participant of a shared contact (0089). Schedules can be inspected and canceled from the chat.

## Initiative

`docs/initiative.md`. Durable reviews decide whether a contact waits, adds a thought, starts a later conversation or stops (0032, 0033, 0065, 0067). Quiet hours 22:00–08:00, limits of 3 per contact and 12 per account per 24 hours, a 24-hour continuation horizon and a separate seven-day budget for new conversations. Since 0094, generated and first-circle contacts send one welcome message before the person writes (`welcome` job, `dispatchContactWelcomes`), and quiet chats get re-engagement episodes after about 1, 3 and 7 days, up to three unanswered spontaneous messages since the person last wrote. Each participant can disable it per contact.

## Budgets and limits

`docs/agentic-limits.md` keeps the single table of rounds, executions and research budgets per turn type. Highlights:

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Tool rounds per reply | 3 (2 for schedules) |
| Queued or running turns per private chat | 10 |
| Chat generation rate | 20 per minute per user |
| Group energy | 50 per contact, +1 every 5 minutes; 100 bot messages per exchange |
| Work turns | 6 per task, 30 per contact per day |
| Web research | 10 per account and 100 per installation per day |
| Initiative | 3 per contact and 12 per account per day |
